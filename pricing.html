Pricing

Priced per acre or per field so you know the cost before the season starts. Every prescription runs on Baseline Rx, with year-end analysis through Agronomy 365.

In-Season Script Add-on

Seed Scripts

Full-Year Baseline Rx Package

Multiple-Depth Zone Sampling

Composite Soil Sampling
$150/ field

In-season & scouting

In-Season Field Sampling
$500/ field

Soil, tissue and grain collection with a scouting report each visit and year-end analysis.

Field Scouting
$125/ hour · $100 min

For fields not enrolled in Baseline Rx that season.

Good to know: Lab fees are not included in soil sampling. Fertility and seed scripts require the Indicator Complete or Indicator Max soil test, and VRI scripts require Baseline Rx Zone Maps. Baseline Rx acres include up to 3 scouting events per year (additional events $100 each). Farms with 50% or more of their acres in Baseline Rx get general pesticide recommendations on non-script acres at no cost. Mileage fees apply beyond 100 miles.
